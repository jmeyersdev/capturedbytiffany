Behind the Lens

A love for design and an eye for detail.

Tiffany is a Gold Coast-based interior photographer, content creator, and the host of ABI Interiors’ House Tours on YouTube. With a love for design and an eye for detail, her photography has been featured in leading architecture and interior design magazines around the world.

As a freelance photographer, Tiffany specialises in capturing homes, interiors, and lifestyle spaces with a refined, editorial eye. Her images are warm, thoughtful, and full of life, created to showcase each space in its best light for designers, architects, and brands.
